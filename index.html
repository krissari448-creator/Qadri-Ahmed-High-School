About the institution

Location and School Hours

Located in Bouira Province, the high school provides a quiet educational environment conducive to focus and academic achievement.

The institution welcome its students weekly from Sunday to Thursday according to the official approved school hours.

Available Academic Streams

Mathematics Stream

Deep focus on logical thinking and mathematical analysis to prepare student for higher engineering and scientific fields.

Experimental Sciences Stream

Solid scientific foundation emphasizing practical lab work and direct analysis in natural sciences and physics.

Mathematical Technique Stream

Comprehensive coverage of mechanical, electrical, and civil engineering branches with practical applications.

Management and Economics Stream

Study of accounting principles, economics, and financial sciences to build a strong foundation in business administration.

Literature and Philosophy Stream

Develpment of intellectual analysis, literary criticism, and deep study of philosophical and humanistic subjects.

Foreign Languages Stream

Enhancing proficiency in living languages, global communication skills, and advanced literary expression.

Laboratories and Educational Facilities

Specialized Scientific Laboratories

Natural science and physics laboratories fully equipped for all prescribed practical experiments.

Computer Laboratories

Digital hardware and software dedicated for teaching computer science and technical applications.

School Library

A quiet, well-equipped space containing curriculom references, scientific books, and literary resources for reading and research.

Sports Facilities

Frequently Asked Questions and Administrative Details

What are the official school days !

Classes take place regularly from Sunday to Thursday, strictly following the official timetable established by the Ministry of National Education.

Does the school offer practical lab work for scientific streams !

Yes, the school is fully equipped with all necessary laboratories and tools to apply theoretical lessons in experimental sciences, physics, and technology accurately.

How are parents received by the administration !

Specific reception hours are scheduled for parents, updated regularly to review students' academic progress and conduct with administrative and teaching staff.
